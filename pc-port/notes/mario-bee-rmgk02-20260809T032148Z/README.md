# MarioBee RMGK02 reconstruction evidence

## Scope

- Reconstructed `src/Game/Player/MarioBee.cpp` from the RMGK02 retail assembly in
  `build/RMGK02/asm/Game/Player/MarioBee.s`.
- Did not edit `Mario.hpp`, any other shared header, PC activation code, build
  configuration, or protected/user-owned files.
- Audio calls are retained where they are part of retail control flow; this work
  does not add or activate a PC audio implementation.

## Recovered behavior

- Bee stamina refill while grounded under the retail movement/status gates.
- Honey-wall entry setup: alpha mode, position, gravity/head/front vectors,
  animation and walk reset, blend timer, movement flag, and shadow state.
- Complete airborne Bee behavior: recovery timers, wall-jump animation
  transition, gravity-component separation/restoration, horizontal steering,
  flight activation, vertical/horizontal threshold selection, stamina drain and
  exhaustion, facing updates, randomized lift, vertical clamps, powered-flight
  gravity handling, and free-fall recovery.
- All ten local `NrvMarioActor` singleton initializers.

The Japanese string offsets were checked against the retail Shift-JIS blob at
`lbl_805C67E0`: `ハチ壁ジャンプ`, `ハチ飛行中`, `落下`,
`ハチ飛行中無入力`, `ハチ体力切れ`, `ハチセブン空中`,
`空中ふんばり`, and `ハチジャンプ`.

The apparently unused result of `jumpDirection.dot(getWorldPadDir())` is
intentional: the retail instruction stream performs the dot product in that
branch but does not consume its result. It is preserved rather than replaced
with speculative behavior.

## Evidence and remaining diff

- Primary evidence: symbolized RMGK02 assembly and the linked retail-equivalent
  ELF/DOL.
- `ghidra_decompile.py` records the headless Ghidra query used for the three
  non-static routines. Ghidra produced useful pseudocode for the two short
  functions; its decompiler could not lift the large airborne function because
  of paired-single instructions.
- m2c at commit `0d49bba7706469ec73f09a40bb710e37c6c2a2d6` was used as a secondary
  control-flow aid. Every branch, field access, call, and constant in the large
  routine was then checked manually against the retail assembly.

The remaining `Mario::beeMarioOnAir()` objdiff consists of compiler allocation
identity only: the external retail string-blob relocation versus the local
translation-unit string object, local `.sdata2` symbol identities, several
equivalent temporary stack slots/registers, and one equivalent floating-point
register choice. Instruction operations, branch structure, function size, and
observable behavior align.
